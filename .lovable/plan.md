

# CKIM Homes & Estates - Real Estate Platform

A premium real estate website for the Rwanda market and diaspora clients, featuring property listings, best deals, team showcase, and a complete admin dashboard.

---

## 🎨 Design Foundation

**Brand Identity:**
- **Primary:** Deep Green (#0F3D2E)
- **Secondary:** Gold (#C9A24D)  
- **Neutrals:** White, Light Gray (#F4F4F4), Dark Gray (#333333)
- **Typography:** Poppins for headings, Inter for body text
- **Style:** Luxury, modern African real estate brand

---

## 📱 Public Website Pages

### 1. Home Page
- Header with logo and navigation (Home, Properties, Best Deals, Team, About, Contact)
- "List Property" CTA button
- Hero section with stunning Rwanda property imagery
- **Auto-sliding Best Deals carousel** with gold "HOT OFFER" badges
- Featured properties grid (6 property cards)
- "Why Choose Us" trust section
- WhatsApp CTA section
- Footer with contact info and social links

### 2. Properties Listing Page
- Search bar by location
- Filter sidebar: Rent/Sale toggle, price range slider, property type
- Property cards grid with pagination
- Sort options (price, newest, etc.)

### 3. Property Details Page
- Multi-image gallery/carousel
- Property title, price, and location
- Full description
- Feature icons (bedrooms, bathrooms, parking, size)
- Agent contact buttons (Call & WhatsApp)
- Similar properties section

### 4. Best Deals Page
- Dedicated page for highlighted deals
- Gold badges and emphasized pricing
- Grid layout with premium card styling

### 5. Team Page
- Hero section introducing the team
- Team member cards (photo, name, position, bio, contact icons)
- Featured founder/leader section
- Company values section

### 6. About Us Page
- Company story and mission
- Vision for Rwanda & diaspora real estate
- Trust and credibility messaging
- Key statistics or achievements

### 7. Contact Page
- Contact form (name, email, phone, message)
- Direct phone and WhatsApp buttons
- Email address
- Map placeholder for office location
- Social media links
